# Creed

Most software is a mouth. It takes the day, then rents your attention back as a feed, a streak, a score.

Aperture is a margin. It sits beside the day and does not take it.

What becomes real is not the sum of your possibilities. It is what remains after you refuse the rest. Name the refusal. A road not taken is a fact.

The room will not tell you who to be. It will show you who you have already been choosing.

Its only intelligence is an earlier sentence of yours. If a later version is allowed to speak with a model, the model stays optional, keyed by you, and silent until asked. It may not replace the archive, summarize it away, or keep a copy.

Memory stays on the device. Export is the whole life of the room. Import replaces nothing until you confirm it.

There is no public profile. A gap is not a failure. Absence is allowed.

Share the file. Do not share the record.
